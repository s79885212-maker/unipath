## Interpretation safeguards

- Tuition and fixed or per-credit fees never establish a full annual budget. Historical UC3M/Bologna figures and undated UAB/Polytechnique prices are not Fall2027 full-cost matches.
- UC3M IELTS5.5 is a first-year language-certification rule, not a verified entry cutoff. IE IELTS7.0 is the minimum;7.5 is a recommendation. Section minima remain unknown where not published.
- SAT/ACT unknowns stay unknown. Bocconi accepts its own test/SAT/ACT; IE academic-test alternatives are explicit. ESSEC optional SAT support does not establish optional ACT.
- IE application rounds are separate from decision communication; no closing hour is invented. Some pending assessments/documents may follow under the source FAQ. Bocconi winter deadline is15:00 Europe/Rome. Polytechnique conflicting intake heading is flagged and not placed in the confirmed calendar.
- EPITA French B2 is a graduation condition, not a B2 admission prerequisite. ESSEC Cergy does not inherit Rabat-only language alternatives.
- Full living budgets and individual grant entitlement remain unknown. No competitive aid is deducted. Photos use existing standard placeholders; no unlicensed or generated campus photos are added.
